# Facilitator pack


## Roles and protocol

Percival facilitates; Durvank observes. Only the facilitator talks to the volunteer during tasks. Read the script and task cards word for word.

## Session flow (15 minutes)

| **Minutes** | **Step**                                   | **Maximum** |
|-------------|--------------------------------------------|-------------|
| 0–2         | Welcome, consent, how the session works    | 2 min       |
| 2–3         | Background question                        | 1 min       |
| 3–4         | Volunteer skims printed target document    | 1 min       |
| 4–8         | Task 1: upload the document                | 4 min       |
| 8–9         | Feedback form, questions 1 and 2 only      | 1 min       |
| 9–13        | Task 2: find the document again            | 4 min       |
| 13–15       | Feedback form, questions 3 to 5; thank you | 2 min       |

## Opening script

Thanks for helping. KnowledgeVault is a tool for keeping your documents and finding them again later by describing what they're about. We're testing the app, not you, so there are no wrong answers. If something is confusing, that's useful for us to know. Please think out loud as you go. I can't help during the tasks, so just do what you'd normally do. Is it okay if we take notes? We won't write down your name.

## Background question

How do you keep track of documents and notes today?

## Before Task 1

Hand over the printed document: “Take a minute to skim this. It’s a document you want to keep.” Then hand over the Task 1 card.

## Between tasks

Take the printed document away. Give the volunteer feedback form questions 1 and 2. Then hand over the Task 2 card.

## Closing

“That’s everything. Thank you. Now three last questions.” Hand over the rest of the feedback form, then answer any questions they have.

## Ground rules

1.  Read the script and task cards word for word.

2.  Do not help. If the volunteer asks “is this right?”, answer “What would you expect to happen?”

3.  Allowed prompts: “What are you thinking?” and “Please do what you would do if I weren’t here.”

4.  Stop a task at four minutes or when the volunteer says they are done, whichever is first. Say “Thanks, that’s helpful” and move on.

5.  Never explain a feature, even after the task ends, until the feedback form is finished.

6.  Volunteers appear only as P1, P2 and so on. Do not write down names, emails or anything from their own files.
