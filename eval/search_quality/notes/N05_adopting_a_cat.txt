Bringing a new cat home

Set up one quiet room first, with food, water, a litter box in a corner far from the bowls, a hiding place such as a covered bed or a box, and a scratching post. Let the cat settle in that room for a few days before it explores the rest of the house.

Do not force attention. Sit on the floor, let the cat come to you, and offer treats. Most cats take a week or two to relax, and some hide under the bed for the first days, which is normal.

Book a vet visit within the first week for a check-up, vaccines and a microchip. Use the same food the shelter used at first, and change it gradually over a week to avoid an upset stomach.

If you already have a pet, introduce them slowly: swap bedding so they learn each other's smell, then let them meet through a baby gate for short sessions.
