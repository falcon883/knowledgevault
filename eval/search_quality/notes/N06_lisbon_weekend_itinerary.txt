Lisbon in two days: notes for our trip

Day one: start in the Alfama, the old hilly quarter, and walk up to the Sao Jorge castle for the view over the red roofs and the river. Ride the yellow tram 28 early, before the queues, and stop for pasteis de nata, the custard tarts, at a bakery in Belem in the afternoon. Visit the Belem Tower and the monastery next door. Dinner: grilled sardines and a glass of vinho verde.

Day two: take the train from Rossio station to Sintra, about forty minutes, to see the colourful Pena Palace and the gardens. Go early, since the buses up the hill fill fast, and wear comfortable shoes. Back in the city, spend the evening listening to fado in a small tavern.

Tips: the metro and trams use a rechargeable card, the pavements are slippery stone, and most museums close on Mondays.
