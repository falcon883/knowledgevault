Preparing for a job interview

Spend an hour on the company before the interview: what it sells, who its customers are, and one recent piece of news. Read the job description line by line and note an example from your own experience for each requirement.

Most behavioural questions, such as "tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate" or "describe a project that went wrong", are best answered with the STAR method. Describe the Situation, the Task you were responsible for, the Action you took, and the Result, ideally with a number. Keep each answer to about two minutes, and own the mistakes: interviewers want to hear what you learned.

Prepare two or three questions to ask them, for example how success is measured in the first six months. Send a short thank-you email the same day, and mention one specific thing you discussed.
